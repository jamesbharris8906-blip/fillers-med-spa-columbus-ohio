What fillers do, in plain words

Fillers are soft gels, most often made with hyaluronic acid, a substance your skin already makes. A provider places small amounts under the skin to add volume where it has faded. Lips gain shape, cheeks gain lift, and folds near the mouth look softer. At Fillers med spa Columbus Ohio, the team uses small amounts first, checks the shape with you in a mirror, and adds more only if you both agree it helps.

Fillers are not the same as Botox. Botox relaxes muscles that make lines, while fillers add volume and shape. Many people use both, because lines and lost volume often show up together. Your provider will say which one fits each spot on your face, and which spots are best left alone. That mix-and-match plan is part of facial balancing, a service that looks at the whole face at once.

A filler visit, step by step

Treatments at the spa

Botox

Botox relaxes small muscles that cause forehead lines, frown lines, and crow's feet. It pairs well with fillers, because it smooths lines while fillers restore shape. The team often plans both in one visit for a balanced result.

Lip Filler

Lip filler is the most asked-for filler here. A soft gel adds shape to the lip edge, a touch of fullness, and better balance between the top and bottom lip. The team adds it slowly and checks the look with you at each step.

Facial Balancing

Facial balancing is a full-face filler plan. Instead of treating one spot, the provider looks at cheeks, chin, jaw, and lips together and places small amounts where they help most. The goal is a face that looks even and rested.

Microneedling

Microneedling uses fine needles to wake up collagen and improve skin texture over time. It does not add volume, but it helps the skin over fillers look smoother. Many guests space it a few weeks from a filler visit.

Cheek Filler

Cheek filler restores lift in the middle of the face. It can soften smile lines below, make the face look less tired, and give a gentle lift to the lower face. The team shapes it to fit your bone structure, not a fixed mold.

Diamond Glow Facial

The Diamond Glow Facial cleans, buffs, and feeds the skin with a serum picked for you. It is a good prep step before fillers, because clean, bright skin shows off shape better. Most guests leave with a fresh glow the same day.

Xeomin

Xeomin is a shot like Botox that relaxes the muscles behind frown lines. Some guests pick it for a simple, clean option. It can sit well in a plan that also uses fillers for volume in other areas.

Microneedling With Exosomes

This treatment pairs microneedling with exosomes to support healing and skin renewal. It helps tone and texture over the weeks after a visit. Guests who want more from microneedling often ask for this version.

Hair Restoration Microneedling

Hair restoration microneedling treats the scalp with fine needles to wake up hair roots. It is a slow, gentle path for thinning hair, and the team talks through the months ahead so hopes stay real. It uses the same care as a facial visit.

Chin Filler

Chin filler adds length or shape to the chin and can fix a small dip or weak side view. It also helps lips and jaw look more balanced. The provider checks the shape from the front and the side before finishing.

Jawline Filler

Jawline filler adds a clear, firm line along the jaw. It can make a soft jaw look stronger and help the lower face match the cheeks and chin. The team places it in small amounts for a clean, natural edge.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do fillers last?

Most fillers last six to twelve months, and lip fillers often last a bit less. The exact time depends on the product, the area, and how fast your body clears it. Your provider can set a touch-up date before the shape fades.

Do fillers hurt?

Most people feel a sting and some pressure, and numbing cream helps a lot. Lips tend to feel more tender than cheeks or chin. The visit is short, and the team goes at your pace and checks in often.

Will my lips look fake or too big?

Not with a careful plan. The team starts with a small amount, checks the shape with you, and stops at a natural point. If you want a very soft change, say so at the start, and they will keep the dose light.

How much filler will I need?

It depends on the area and the change you want. Lips may need one syringe, while cheeks or a full-face balance may need more. The team gives you a clear quote after the free consult, so there are no surprises.

Can fillers be undone?

Yes, most hyaluronic acid fillers can be dissolved with an enzyme if needed. That safety net helps many first-timers feel calmer. Your provider will explain when that step is used and what it involves.

What is the difference between Botox and fillers?

Botox relaxes muscles that make lines, and fillers add volume and shape. Botox suits forehead and eye lines, while fillers suit lips, cheeks, chin, and jaw. Many people use both, in different spots, for a full result.
